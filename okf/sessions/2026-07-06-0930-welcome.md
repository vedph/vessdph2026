# Abstract

Franz Fischer and the VeDPH team welcome participants to the Venice Summer School in Digital and Public Humanities. The opening session introduces the programme, its thematic areas, and the practical organisation of the week, while outlining the Centre’s interdisciplinary approach to digital scholarship, cultural heritage, and public engagement. It also offers participants an opportunity to meet the teaching team, become familiar with the spaces and resources of the VeDPH, and begin the conversations and collaborations that will develop throughout the Summer School.

# About the speaker

Franz Fischer is Associate Professor of Medieval and Humanist Latin Literature at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice and Founding Director of the Venice Centre for Digital and Public Humanities. His research lies at the intersection of medieval studies, digital philology, scholarly editing, and digital cultural heritage. Before joining Ca’ Foscari in 2019, he worked at the Royal Irish Academy in Dublin and served as coordinator of the Cologne Center for eHumanities, where he contributed to and coordinated several international Digital Humanities projects. He is a founding member of the Institute for Documentology and Scholarly Editing, Co-Director of the CLARIN Knowledge Centre for Digital and Public Textual Scholarship, and Editor-in-Chief of magazén – International Journal for Digital and Public Humanities.
