# Recommended reading

- Sahle, Patrick. “What Is a Scholarly Digital Edition?” In Digital Scholarly Editing: Theories and Practices, edited by Matthew James Driscoll and Elena Pierazzo, 19-40. Cambridge: Open Book Publishers, 2016. https://doi.org/10.11647/OBP.0095.02 https://www.openbookpublishers.com/books/10.11647/obp.0095/chapters/10.11647/obp.0095.02 — A foundational discussion of what distinguishes a born-digital scholarly edition from a digitised print edition.
- Buzzoni, Marina. “A Protocol for Scholarly Digital Editions? The Italian Point of View.” In Digital Scholarly Editing: Theories and Practices, edited by Matthew James Driscoll and Elena Pierazzo, 59-82. Cambridge: Open Book Publishers, 2016. https://doi.org/10.11647/OBP.0095.04 https://www.openbookpublishers.com/books/10.11647/obp.0095/chapters/10.11647/obp.0095.04 — Examines the defining features and scholarly added value of digital editions, including interconnected data, multimedia content, critical apparatuses, and interoperability.
- Pierazzo, Elena. “Modelling Digital Scholarly Editing: From Plato to Heraclitus.” In Digital Scholarly Editing: Theories and Practices, edited by Matthew James Driscoll and Elena Pierazzo, 41.58. Cambridge: Open Book Publishers, 2016. https://doi.org/10.11647/OBP.0095.03 https://www.openbookpublishers.com/books/10.11647/obp.0095/chapters/10.11647/obp.0095.03 — Introduces digital editing as a modelling process and discusses the relationship between documents, texts, interpretation, and scholarly representation.
- Burgio, Eugenio, Marina Buzzoni, and Samuela Simion, eds. Digital Edition of the Devisement dou monde: A Comprehensive Digital Scholarly Edition. Critical Translation and Commentary. Venice: Edizioni Ca’ Foscari, 2024. https://doi.org/10.30687/978-88-6969-872-9 https://edizionicafoscari.unive.it/en/edizioni4/libri/978-88-6969-872-9/ — The principal case study discussed during the session: a multilingual digital critical edition combining the textual tradition, critical translation, variants, commentary, semantic annotation, and EVT visualisation.
- Grieves, Michael, and John Vickers. “Digital Twin: Mitigating Unpredictable, Undesirable Emergent Behavior in Complex Systems.” In Transdisciplinary Perspectives on Complex Systems: New Findings and Approaches, edited by Franz-Josef Kahlen, Shannon Flumerfelt, and Anabela Alves, 85-113. Cham: Springer, 2017. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-38756-7_4 https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-38756-7_4 — The original theoretical reference used during the lecture to introduce the concept of the digital twin and the relationship between a physical object and its evolving digital representation.
