# Abstract

The guided tour of the Venice State Archives introduces participants to one of Europe’s most important archival institutions and to the practices through which documentary heritage is preserved, described, and made accessible for research. Led by the Director, Andrea Erboso, together with Federico Diamanti and Monica Del Rio, the visit offers an overview of the Archive’s history, organisation, collections, and research services.

The tour concludes with an exhibition of selected documents relating to Giacomo Casanova, Marco Polo, and Veronica Franco. These materials provide a direct encounter with the different voices, genres, and institutional contexts preserved in the Archive, from administrative records to documents capable of restoring individual experience and agency. Through these examples, participants are invited to reflect on the relationship between archival description, historical interpretation, and the materiality of documentary sources, as well as on the role of archives in shaping cultural memory and enabling new research questions.
