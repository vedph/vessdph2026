# About the speaker

Peter Robinson is Professor of English at the University of Saskatchewan. His research focuses on textual scholarship, digital scholarly editing, manuscript traditions, and the use of computational methods to study texts preserved in multiple versions. He has developed several tools for the creation and publication of digital editions and leads the development of Textual Communities, a collaborative platform for transcribing, editing, and exploring complex textual traditions. He is particularly known for his long-standing work on Geoffrey Chaucer’s Canterbury Tales and has also contributed to digital editions of texts in Old Norse, Armenian, Greek, and Italian traditions.
