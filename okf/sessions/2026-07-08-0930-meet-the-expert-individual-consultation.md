# Abstract

The 'Pimp my Project' sessions turn the School's expertise towards the participants' own work. Rather than following a taught strand, participants bring their individual research projects (at any stage, from a first idea to a working prototype) and discuss them one-to-one with expert teachers from across the School's domains: digital archaeology, textual scholarship, history, art history, and the methods and technologies that connect them. The consultations are deliberately dialogic and formative: a chance to sharpen a research question, rethink a data model, weigh tools and standards, test a project's scope and feasibility, and map out concrete next steps. Each participant leaves with situated, practical feedback on the questions that matter most to their own research.

Participants gather at 9:30 at the VeDPH Lab and then divide into four parallel strands:

Strand 1 - VeDPH Lab (2nd floor)
Strand 2 - Sala Geymonat (1st floor)
Strand 3 - Sala Morelli (ground floor)
Strand 4 - Common Room
