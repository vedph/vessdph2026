# Abstract

This session will provide an introduction to one of the important tools and methodologies of spatial analysis, namely GIS. In this workshop, we will cover the components of the methodology and students will learn how to turn humanities data into a GIS project. The session will end with a crowd-sourcing activity in the streets of Venice.

# About the speaker

Amanda Madden is an assistant professor of history at George Mason University in Fairfax, Virginia and affiliate faculty at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media. She is the author of Civil Blood: Vendetta Violence and the Civic Elites in Early Modern Italy and the project director of Mapping Violence in Early Modern Italy.
