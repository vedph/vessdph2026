# Abstract

In this session, we will explore the history of Venetian space and place through the lens of early modeern violence. During this session, students will be introduced to the basics of spatial analysis with GIS while looking at the various components of this methodology including data collection and analysis. We will end our session with a crowdsourcing public history activity using GIS.

# About the speaker

Amanda Madden is an assistant professor of history at George Mason University in Fairfax, Virginia. She is the author of the book Civil Blood: Vendetta Violence and the Civic Elites in Early Modern Italy and the project director of Mapping Violence in Early Modern Italy.
