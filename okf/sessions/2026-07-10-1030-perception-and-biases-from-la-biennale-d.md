# Abstract

This workshop asks how we perceive and come to understand a work of contemporary art, and what becomes of that understanding once it is routed through a generative AI model that turns written language into images. Over two sessions, participants encounter unfamiliar works at the Biennale and photograph one, then later try to regenerate it with a text to image system. The aim is not to produce a convincing copy but to externalise an interpretation: to make visible, in a new image, how a single viewer has understood the original.
