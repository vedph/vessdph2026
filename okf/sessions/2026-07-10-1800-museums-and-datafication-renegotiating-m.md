# Abstract

One of the many challenges that museums face today is to develop infrastructures for a post-digital condition that assumes data will be remixed, in motion and capable of flowing without friction across platforms and devices. This demands an agile, hybridised approach to cultural heritage content that by offering digital content ready for re-use across a network, enables the user to have increased agency and become active in the world. In this keynote presentation, Ross Parry and Vince Dziekan will discuss how museums might renegotiate their digital interfaces in order to facilitate an expanding range of user interactions that are diverse, open-ended and on-going; in other words, for content that can be experienced in hybrid ways by being multi-modal, multi-format and multi-purpose – all at once.

# About the speaker

Ross Parry is Professor of Museum Technology, in the School of Heritage and Culture at the University of Leicester. From 2020 to 2025 he served on the Steering Committee of the Arts and Humanities Research Council’s ‘Towards a National Collection’ UK digital infrastructure initiative. He is co-investigator of ‘The Sensational Museum’ research project, and with Vince Dziekan, he co-edits the Routledge book series ‘Critical Perspectives on Museums and Digital Technology’. He is a Principal Fellow of the Higher Education Academy, Advisory Board member for Attenborough Arts Centre, a former Tate Research Fellow, former chair of the UK’s national Museums Computer Group, and is currently serving as Chair of the Editorial Board of the Science Museum Journal (Science Museum Group, UK). In 2022 he became the founding Director of the new Institute for Digital Culture. With Art UK and Collections Trust, the Institute is founding partner of the new national Museum Data Service – supported by UKRI and Bloomberg Philanthropies. A widely published author, his recent books include: ‘Museums and Digital Confidence’ (Routledge 2025), co-edited with Vince Dziekan and Karin de Wild; ‘Museums and the History of Computing’ (Routledge, 2024), co-edited with Simone Natale and Petrina Foti; and ‘The Routledge Handbook of Museums, Media and Communication’ (Routledge, 2019), co-edited with Kirsten Drotner, Vince Dziekan, and Kim Schrøder.

Vince Dziekan is a senior academic and practitioner-researcher at Monash Art, Design and Architecture (MADA), Monash University, Australia, and an honorary research fellow of the Institute for Digital Culture at the University of Leicester, UK. Vince’s work engages with the transformation of contemporary curatorial practices at the intersection of emerging design practices, creative technology and museum culture. The scope of these interdisciplinary investigations is reflected in his books, Virtuality and the Art of Exhibition: Curatorial Design for the Multimedial Museum (Intellect Books and University of Chicago Press, 2012), The Routledge Handbook of Museums, Media and Communication (co-edited with Kirsten Drotner, Ross Parry and Kim Christian Schrøder; Routledge, 2018) and Museums and Digital Confidence (co-edited with Ross Parry and Karin de Wild; Routledge, 2025) and Museums and design practices (co-authored with Marco Mason; Routledge, 2025). Vince has also published widely in non-traditional modes through his independent curatorial practice, including as founding curator of MWX, the exhibition initiative of Museums and the Web (United States), and through various forms of applied and translational research in the field, including as a chief investigator for Future Museum (Austria).
